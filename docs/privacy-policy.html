Lista – Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2026

Lista ("we", "our", or "the app") is a shared shopping list and recipe app for households. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account information: Email address and password (when signing up with email), or your Apple ID name and email (when using Sign in with Apple).
  • User content: Shopping lists, items, recipes, meal plans, and any images you upload.
  • Household data: Household membership and invite links you create or accept.
  • Photos: Only if you choose to import a recipe image from your photo library. We do not access your camera or photos without your explicit action.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and sync your shopping lists and recipes across household members.
  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • To allow household members you invite to access shared content.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on Supabase servers (EU region). Supabase is compliant with GDPR and uses industry-standard encryption at rest and in transit.

4. Data Sharing

Your data is shared only with members of your household who you explicitly invite. We do not share your data with third parties except as required to operate the service (Supabase infrastructure).

5. Sign in with Apple

If you use Sign in with Apple, we receive only the information Apple provides (name and email). You may choose to hide your email using Apple's private relay feature. We store only what is necessary to identify your account.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete account in the app. This permanently deletes all your data, including shared lists and recipes, within 24 hours.

7. Children's Privacy

Lista is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To request a data export or raise a privacy concern, contact us at the email below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
