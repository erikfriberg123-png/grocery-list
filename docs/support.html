Frequently Asked Questions

How do I invite someone to my household?

Open the app, go to any list, and tap the share icon. You can copy the invite link or show the QR code for others to scan.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and all associated data.

The app is not syncing — what do I do?

Make sure you have an active internet connection. Close and reopen the app. If the problem persists, sign out and sign back in.
